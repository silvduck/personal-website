About Me

I am a cybersecurity specialist at ITechGrup by Plexus Tech and a PhD researcher at the University of Lleida, in the Cryptography and Graphs research group.

My work focuses on post-quantum cryptography, Zero Trust architectures, digital identity and privacy. I work at the intersection of cybersecurity engineering, research and innovation, with a particular interest in how cryptographic systems can remain secure in the quantum era.

I hold a BSc in Computer Engineering from the Autonomous University of Barcelona (UAB) and an MSc in Cybersecurity and Privacy from the Open University of Catalonia (UOC). I also enjoy communicating complex technology through talks and outreach activities.
