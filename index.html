About

I'm a cybersecurity specialist at ITechGrup by Plexus and a PhD researcher in the Cryptography & Graphs Research Group at the University of Lleida.

My work spans applied cryptography, security architecture, identity and access management, and the design of secure systems. I have contributed to R&D initiatives involving post-quantum cryptography, including the INCIBE-supported Quantum Zero Trust (QZT) project, with a focus on bringing emerging cryptographic solutions into real-world environments.
